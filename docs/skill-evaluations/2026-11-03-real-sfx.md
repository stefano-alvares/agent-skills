## Claim and mechanism

Claim: sound design for motion videos with real recorded sounds, never synthesised. It places sounds on the exact
frame, keeps music and voice, and returns a new MP4, a separate SFX WAV, a cue report and a self-critique.

Mechanism: Python scripts (stdlib + numpy) and FFmpeg. `build_index.py` measures every sound in the configured
libraries and gives it a role from its folder and name. `from_cues.py` turns an event list into a cue sheet.
`mix.py` picks or takes named sounds, levels them by role and perceived loudness, ducks them under a voice stem, then
masters and muxes. `verify.py` and `critique.py` score the sounds as numbers (sync, pacing, hierarchy, speech, mono and
more) and print a listen list. The agent cannot hear, and the skill says so.

Required conditions: Python 3.9+, FFmpeg with `drawtext` and `rubberband` (both present here), a sound library.
It ships only uisfx (78 synthesised UI cues). Network use is opt-in only: `listen.py install` (YAMNet, 16 MB),
`hear.py install` (3.1 GB model), `setup.py --uisfx` (npm). None were run.

## Evidence and unknowns

- Real run on the MiniCardiac Invite how-to (19.3 s, voice + pad music). The library is 23 CC0 Openverse recordings,
  `minicardiac-marketing/content/reels/sfx-library/openverse-cc0/`. Ten events came from the project's beats.
  Round 3: 10/10 sounds on their frame, every critique axis 8+, final −14.1 LUFS, video stream byte-identical. A rebuild
  from the saved sheets gave the same samples.
- Defect found: SKILL.md shows `top SHEET keep_audio=no 'base_audio=[...]'`, but in `mix.py` (`keep = keep_audio !=
  "no"` when a base list is given) that drops the base completely. Round 1 came out with no voice, and the critique still
  scored hook, sync and pacing 10. Its only clue was the line "this film has no sound of its own". Use `keep_audio=auto`.
- A cue's `file` resolves against the working folder, while `video` and `base_audio` resolve against the sheet. Use
  absolute paths for cue files.
- `from_cues.py` printed `BAKED:` on a base with no effects in it: the voice is loud at the tap moments. It was a false
  alarm (0 % onsets at the events against 5 % by chance).
- The auto-pick gave four taps four different click recordings. That breaks the skill's own "one interface family"
  rule. The picks were fixed by hand.
- UNVERIFIED: whether it sounds better. No person has listened yet. The selftest (189 checks, about 5 minutes) was not
  run.

## Existing overlap

`motion-reels` (studio reels) and `minicardiac-product-reels` set the audio rules but place nothing. Fish Audio covers
voice only. No duplicate capability.

## Disproof test

The claim fails if a person, listening blind, prefers the Revision 2 Invite mix (sounds placed by a fixed rule) or flags
the real-sfx mix as loud, buried or extra at its listen-list moments (4.95, 7.55, 15.05 s). Most likely failure here:
the library is thin (23 sounds), so the picker has little to choose from.

## Decision

Trial. It stays installed (Stefano asked for it) and is the placement tool for MiniCardiac reels, with the local rules
in `minicardiac-product-reels/SKILL.md`: `--ui library`, `keep_audio=auto`, voice stem as `key_only`, one click
family chosen by hand. Timebox: the next two MiniCardiac reels. Success: Stefano or Aruba hear no fault at the listen
list moments and prefer it to the fixed-rule mix.
